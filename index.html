Common questions

Houston ADU questions,
answered

How much does an ADU cost in Houston?

Most ADUs in Houston cost between $80,000 and $240,000. A basic garage conversion starts around $80K, while a fully detached two-bedroom cottage runs $200K–$240K+.

How long does it take to build an ADU in Houston?

A garage conversion typically takes 6–10 weeks. A fully detached ADU usually takes 4–6 months from permit approval to move-in ready.

Do I need a permit to build an ADU in Houston?

Yes. Houston allows ADUs up to 900 square feet with proper setbacks. We handle all permits and inspections.

Can my HOA stop me from building an ADU?

Maybe — every HOA is different. We review your community's restrictions and find the cleanest compliant approach.

What size ADU is allowed in Houston?

Houston caps ADUs at 900 square feet with full kitchen and bathroom.

How much can I rent an ADU for in Houston?

Most Houston ADUs rent for $1,200–$2,500/month.
