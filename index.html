Common questions

Questions,
answered

What can I build in my backyard in Houston?

Whatever you can imagine. We build detached ADUs, tiny homes, backyard studios, home offices, in-law suites, guest houses, pool houses, saunas, home gyms, man caves, she sheds, and kids play houses. Every build is designed around your lot, your needs, and how you actually live.

How custom is the build?

100% custom. We don’t work from a catalog or a fixed menu of options. You tell us what you want, we design it, you approve the plan and the price, then we build it. Size, layout, finishes, and features are all up to you.

When do I get a price?

We give you a free written estimate after a free site visit. We walk your lot, talk through what you want, and put together a detailed quote — no pressure, no obligation. We don’t publish a price list because every build is different.

Do I need a permit to build in my backyard in Houston?

Most projects need a permit. Houston allows ADUs up to 900 square feet with proper setbacks. We handle all permits, drawings, and inspections for you.

How long does a custom build take?

Most projects take 3-6 months from design start to move-in ready, depending on size, complexity, and what the city needs to approve. We give you a realistic timeline during the design phase.

Can my HOA stop me from building?

Maybe — every HOA is different. We review your community’s restrictions and find the cleanest compliant approach.
